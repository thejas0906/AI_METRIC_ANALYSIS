## Executive Summary

The initial audit identified three distinct result sets:
- **Result A:** $\text{TP}=12, \text{FP}=3, \text{FN}=10, \text{TN}=59$, Precision $=0.8000$, Recall $=0.5455$, $\text{F1}=0.6486$, Accuracy $=0.8452$ (29 SUP, 15 CON, 40 UNV).

### The Core Question
Why does `results/multiclaim_results.json` record **Result C**, while the calibration records and all 5 publication figures in `results/final_figures/` display **Result A**?

### The Root-Cause Discovery
Through a line-by-line git diff of commit `b948270` ("Final Graphs", Oct 7, 2026), the exact cause was established:
1. **The Evidence Retriever Was Upgraded:** In commit `b948270`, [`retrieval/evidence_retriever.py`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/retrieval/evidence_retriever.py) was enhanced to scan full Wikipedia article bodies (first 40 paragraphs) rather than summary leads alone, using sliding 1-to-3 sentence windows and token-coverage priority boosts.
2. **Result C Was Generated by This Upgraded Retriever:** Running the upgraded pipeline produced `results/multiclaim_results.json` (Result C), which dramatically reduced unverifiable claims ($40 \rightarrow 19$) and boosted F1 from $0.6486$ to $0.7317$.
3. **Result A Was Preserved in Figures Because Figure Script Values Were Hardcoded:** [`generate_final_figures.py`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/generate_final_figures.py) hardcoded the calibration metrics from [`results/audit_archive/support_threshold_calibration_results.json`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/results/audit_archive/support_threshold_calibration_results.json) (which was computed before the retriever upgrade using [`precomputed_nli_data.pkl`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/results/audit_archive/precomputed_nli_data.pkl)). The script was not wired to read `multiclaim_results.json` dynamically.

## 4. Root Cause of the Discrepancy

The discrepancy is **NOT** caused by data corruption, random variance, or mismatched datasets:
1. **The claims and labels are identical.**
2. **The NLI model and thresholds are identical.**
3. **The difference was caused by a deliberate enhancement to evidence retrieval:** On October 7, 2026, the retrieval module was enhanced to scan body paragraphs and use sliding multi-sentence windows.
4. **Execution sequence:**
   - *Phase 1:* The author ran threshold calibration on intermediate cached data (`precomputed_nli_data.pkl`), establishing $\tau_{CSS} = 0.30$ and obtaining **Result A**.
   - *Phase 2:* The author generated the publication figures in [`results/final_figures/`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/results/final_figures/) by hardcoding the metrics of **Result A** into [`generate_final_figures.py`](file:///c:/Users/kickg/OneDrive/Documents/AI_METRIC_ANALYSIS/generate_final_figures.py).
   - *Phase 3:* The author upgraded `retrieval/evidence_retriever.py` to include body paragraphs and windowing, and ran `run_experiment.py`, producing `results/multiclaim_results.json` and `results/multiclaim_metrics.csv` (**Result C**).
   - *Phase 4:* Both the upgraded code, `multiclaim_results.json`, and the static figures were committed together in git commit `b948270`. The figures were not re-run against the new JSON output.
